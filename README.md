# Safaricom Financial Analysis: Investment Scorecard

## Project Overview

This project provides a comprehensive financial analysis of Safaricom PLC, a leading telecommunications and fintech company in Africa. The goal is to evaluate Safaricom's financial health and investment potential using a structured approach that mirrors private equity (PE) due diligence.

Key financial statements (Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement) are analyzed to derive crucial financial metrics, which are then used to build an 'Investment Scorecard' for a holistic assessment.

## Methodology

The analysis is structured around three core financial statements and a final investment scorecard:

1.  **Income Statement Analysis**: Calculates key profitability margins (Gross Margin, EBITDA Margin, Net Margin) and interprets their significance for investor evaluation.
2.  **Balance Sheet Analysis**: Computes critical solvency and liquidity ratios (Current Ratio, Debt/Equity, Debt/EBITDA) to assess the company's financial structure and risk.
3.  **Cash Flow Statement Analysis**: Determines Free Cash Flow (FCF) and the Cash Conversion Ratio to evaluate the quality of earnings and the company's ability to generate cash.
4.  **Investment Scorecard**: Aggregates the insights from the three statements into a weighted scoring system, providing an overall 'Verdict' (e.g., Strong Buy, Buy, Hold, Monitor, Avoid).

## Key Financial Metrics & Insights

### Profitability

*   **Revenue**: KES 388,688.9 Billion
*   **Gross Margin**: 74.0%
*   **EBITDA Margin**: 44.3% (Exceptional - top quartile globally)
*   **Net Margin**: 11.8% (Strong - good profitability)

Safaricom demonstrates **exceptional operating profitability**, with an EBITDA margin placing it among the top global performers. The strong net margin further underscores its ability to translate revenue into significant bottom-line profit.

### Leverage & Liquidity

*   **Current Ratio**: 0.19x (Initially appears concerning, but explained by industry business model)
*   **Debt/Equity**: 0.71x
*   **Debt/EBITDA**: 0.93x (Conservative - low debt burden)

Safaricom maintains a **conservative debt profile**, with a Debt/EBITDA ratio well within typical PE investment criteria. While the current ratio of 0.19x might seem alarming, it is characteristic of a **negative working capital business model** common in telecom and fintech, where cash is collected upfront (e.g., M-Pesa deposits, airtime sales) before services are fully rendered or suppliers are paid. This is not indicative of a liquidity crisis but rather a structural feature.

### Cash Flow & Cash Quality

*   **Free Cash Flow (FCF)**: KES 64,662.7 Billion
*   **FCF Margin**: 16.6%
*   **Cash Conversion**: 141.3% (High Quality earnings - profit converts exceptionally well to cash)

Safaricom exhibits **high-quality earnings**, with a cash conversion ratio significantly above 100%. This indicates that its reported profits are strongly backed by actual cash generation, reinforcing the robustness of its financial performance and mitigating any concerns raised by the low current ratio.

## Conclusion

Safaricom PLC demonstrates robust financial health across multiple dimensions. Its exceptional profitability, conservative leverage, and high-quality cash generation position it as a very attractive investment. The seemingly low current ratio is a common and sustainable feature for companies with its business model, effectively mitigated by its strong operating cash flows and predictable recurring revenue. The overall assessment leads to a **STRONG BUY** verdict.
